Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

MCQwave collects the following personal information during user registration:

  • First Name
  • Last Name
  • Email Address
  • Password (securely hashed and protected)
  • Profile Picture (optional, to be implemented in future)

2. How We Collect Information

We collect information solely through user registration on the MCQwave platform. No additional tracking methods are used.

3. How We Use Your Information

The collected information is used for:

  • Personalizing user experience
  • Communication with users regarding webapp updates or support
  • Authentication and account management

4. Data Sharing

MCQwave does not share user data with any third-party services or organizations. Your information remains confidential and is used exclusively within our platform.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We maintain user data as a backup. However, if a user manually deletes their account, all associated data will be permanently removed from our systems.

6. User Rights

MCQwave provides users with the following rights and features:

  • Solve multiple-choice questions (MCQs)
  • View detailed performance analysis
  • Take mock tests
  • See overall ranking based on performance

7. Contact Information

For any privacy-related queries, account issues, or support, please contact us via the email address provided during registration.
