2. Services Description

MCQwave provides practice questions and preparation materials for medical entrance examinations, including:

  • NEET PG (National Eligibility cum Entrance Test for Post Graduates)
  • INI-CET (Institute of National Importance Combined Entrance Test)
  • NEXT (National Exit Test)
  • FMGE (Foreign Medical Graduate Examination)

Our services include:

  • Original practice MCQs created by MCQwave
  • Memory-based previous year questions
  • Detailed explanations and analysis for each question
  • Performance tracking and progress analysis
  • Content sharing features through our Hive platform
  • Deep analysis through our Pomegranate feature
