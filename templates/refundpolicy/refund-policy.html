Refund Policy

1. Eligibility for Refund

Refunds are considered under the following conditions:

  • Technical issues preventing platform usage
  • Significant discrepancies in service promised vs. delivered

2. Refund Request Process

To request a refund:

  1. Contact support via registered email
  2. Provide detailed explanation of issue
  3. Include account and transaction details

3. Non-Refundable Scenarios

Refunds will NOT be processed for:

  • User dissatisfaction with test performance
  • Voluntary account cancellation
  • Misuse of platform features

4. Refund Timeline

Approved refunds will be processed within 7-10 business days.

5. Contact for Queries

For refund-related questions, email: support@mcqwave.com
